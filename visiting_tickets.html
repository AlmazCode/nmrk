Ticket Prices

Entry to the museum is paid at the ticket office or in advance through our partner platform. Prices below apply to a single-day, single-entry visit and are shown in Kazakhstani tenge (KZT).

Standard admission prices
Category Price Notes
Adult 2,000 ₸ Full exhibition access
Student & senior 1,000 ₸ Valid ID required
Child (7–17) 500 ₸ Must be accompanied by an adult
Child (under 7) Free No ticket required
Family (2 adults + 2 children) 4,500 ₸ Sold only at the ticket office

Prices are reviewed annually. Please confirm current prices at the ticket office or on the official museum website before your visit.

Discount Days

Admission is free for all visitors on the first Sunday of every month. War veterans, people with disabilities, and children from orphanages enter free of charge on any day — a valid supporting document must be shown at the entrance.

Group & School Visits

Groups of ten or more people1 receive a 15% discount on the standard adult price when booked at least three business days in advance. School classes travelling with a teacher pay the child rate, and one accompanying adult per ten students enters free.

1 Minimum group size applies per single booking, not per combined family bookings.

To arrange a group visit, call +7 7172 91 90 35 or write to ulttyk_muzei@nmrk.kz at least a week before your preferred date.

How to Buy a Ticket Online

Tickets can be reserved in advance through our official partner, E-History Kazakhstan, to avoid queues on busy weekends.

  1. Open the ticketing partner website and select «National Museum» from the venue list.
  2. Choose your visit date and the number of tickets per category.
  3. Pay for your order using one of the following methods:
    • Bank card (Visa / Mastercard)
    • Kaspi Pay
    • Mobile wallet
  4. Show the confirmation e-mail or SMS code at the entrance turnstile.

Jump to ticket prices or see the definitions of ticket types below.

Ticket Types Explained

Standard ticket
Grants access to all permanent exhibition halls for one calendar day.
Combined ticket
Adds entry to the current temporary exhibition for an extra fee, purchased at the same time as the standard ticket.
Group ticket
A single booking covering ten or more visitors at the discounted group rate, arranged in advance.
