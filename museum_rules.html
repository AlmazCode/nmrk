General Rules of Conduct

The museum is a place where valuable exhibits are stored. Every visitor must follow the established rules. Below are the main codes of conduct that are mandatory for all guests.

Etiquette in Exhibition Halls

Visitors are not allowed to run inside the building, talk loudly, sing, jump, or play. Phone conversations in exhibition halls and during guided tours are also prohibited. Do not insult museum staff or other visitors and do not use physical force.

Maintain silence and respect other guests. If you are accompanied by a child, make sure they do not step on stanchions, turnstiles, railings, or barriers. Children are also forbidden from jumping in elevators to prevent emergency situations.

What every visitor must know

You may pass through the turnstile only with a valid ticket. Littering or defacing museum premises and grounds is strictly forbidden. Smoking, including ENDS (electronic cigarettes), is strictly prohibited.

Without special permission, you may not:

  • Listen to audio equipment through loudspeakers
  • Use amplification devices
  • Play musical instruments
  • Conduct unauthorised tours, lectures, or other activities in exhibition halls
  • Use museum premises for commercial, advertising, or other activities
  • Carry out any form of propaganda or campaigning

Visitor Tip

We recommend checking the museum opening hours in advance. The average visit lasts about 1–2 h. Wear comfortable shoes, but leave your outerwear at the cloakroom.

Prohibited Items

It is strictly forbidden to bring the following categories of items into the museum. Violating these rules may result in denial of entry or a call to the security service.

  1. Outerwear, headwear, and sunglasses
    • Hats, caps, and wide-brimmed hats
    • Hoods on jackets
    • Sunglasses and tinted lenses
  2. Backpacks of any size, large bags
    • Even empty backpacks are not allowed
    • Only small cross-body bags are permitted
  3. Oversized children’s toys, balloons, flowers, branches, umbrellas
  4. Rollerboards, skates, scooters, bicycles, and similar sporting equipment
  5. Food and drinks, including ice cream (a designated eating area is available)

Special attention: pyrotechnic, flammable, chemical, toxic, and poisonous substances, as well as household gas cylinders, are strictly prohibited. The same applies to firearms, sharp objects, and fragile items.

Bringing animals is not allowed. Service dogs with proper documentation are the only exception.

Photography & Video

Without special permission, photography and video recording with any camera or smartphone are forbidden in all halls without exception.

As the sign at the entrance states: Photography is only allowed with administration approval. This rule applies to all devices, including professional cameras.

“A museum is a temple of memory. Respect its rules, and it will reveal its secrets to you.”

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I take photos?

Photography is allowed only with written permission from the administration. Please contact the hall curator.

Can I bring food inside?

No. A designated eating area is located on the ground floor. Bringing ice cream into the museum is also prohibited.

What should I do if I lost something?
